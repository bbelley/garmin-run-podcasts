# Out & Backlog

## Goal and daily workflow

The target is a personal podcast queue for the Garmin Forerunner 255S Music with easy MP3 uploads, automatic played tracking and cache cleanup, and no new recurring podcast-app fee. Pocket Casts stays the place to discover and select episodes. Daily use should require one watch sync on Wi-Fi, with USB reserved for installing app builds.

1. Add an episode to the manual **Run** playlist in Pocket Casts, or drag an MP3 onto a private upload page that reuses the existing Cloudflare feed and upload workflow. Edit the display title if needed. **Add to Run** defaults on for uploads. Pocket Casts can also follow the custom feed if useful. Its Files feature cannot add uploaded files directly to a playlist. [Pocket Casts Playlists](https://support.pocketcasts.com/knowledge-base/playlists/)
2. Press **Sync Now** before or after a run. The watch sends recorded completions, refreshes its queue, reclaims space from completed audio, and downloads missing episodes. The proposed order is the in-progress episode, then pinned uploads, then Pocket Casts playlist order. Include unpinned uploads after the playlist. Keep one in-progress episode plus up to two upcoming episodes within the watch's byte budget and reserve.
3. Start the Garmin's native **Run** activity and listen through the native media player and Bluetooth headphones. Show ready minutes, last sync, and pending status on the watch. **Mark Finished** records a completion. **Delete Download** only removes the local copy and should hold off re-downloading it until explicitly requested. [Garmin Audio Content Providers](https://developer.garmin.com/connect-iq/connect-iq-basics/app-types/)

V1 reads the dedicated playlist and writes verified played completions for matched Pocket Casts episodes. Keep playback bookmarks on the watch and unmatched upload completions in app state. Queue edits, archive changes, and position writes to Pocket Casts are outside v1. **Up Next** can become an optional read source later. **Share → Add to Run** provides an alternate selection path if the private endpoints fail. Exact cross-device resume is optional.

## 1. Prove the two integrations independently

Run these spikes before building Worker infrastructure. Record firmware and SDK versions, observed responses and callbacks, and pass or fail results with sensitive data removed.

### Garmin

On a Forerunner 255S Music, the prototype downloaded and played the 30-second HTTPS clip and counted natural completion. The tester also confirmed that the updated build saved a bookmark, resumed from the provider menu, and handled **Test Next Track**. The update loaded without a power cycle. The [watch test notes](garmin-spike/README.md#verification) distinguish observations from checks still to run.

- Use the Connect IQ SDK and VS Code Monkey C extension. Generate a personal signing key, build a minimal Audio Content Provider for `fr255sm`, and copy the signed `.prg` to `GARMIN/APPS` over USB. Personal sideloading uses the device-build path and needs no public store approval. Use Garmin's Monkey Music sample as a reference. [SDK setup and key generation](https://developer.garmin.com/connect-iq/connect-iq-basics/getting-started/) · [Sideloading](https://developer.garmin.com/connect-iq/connect-iq-basics/your-first-app/) · [Forerunner 255S Music](https://developer.garmin.com/connect-iq/device-reference/fr255sm/) · [Monkey Music sample](https://github.com/garmin/connectiq-apps/tree/master/audio-provider/monkeymusic)
- Finish the hardware test with a longer spoken MP3 from the existing Cloudflare feed. Download it over Wi-Fi, then disconnect Wi-Fi and the phone and play it over Bluetooth during a native Run. Pause several minutes in, reopen playback from the provider menu, and compare the audio with the point where it paused. Reboot with a bookmark set to check persistence. Capture the updated build's `ContentDelegate.onSong()` events for pause, skip, and natural completion. [ContentDelegate](https://developer.garmin.com/connect-iq/api-docs/Toybox/Media/ContentDelegate.html) · [Media event constants](https://developer.garmin.com/connect-iq/api-docs/Toybox/Media.html)
- **Exit criterion.** The sideloaded provider downloads and plays offline on real hardware during a saved Run. It resumes the longer clip at the saved audio position after leaving the player and after reboot. Completion survives reboot and is available at the next sync. Pause and skip produce no completion. Record the firmware and observed callbacks.

### Pocket Casts

The [read-only probe](pocket-casts-spike/README.md) passes local fixture checks. Run it against a signed-in account now, alongside the remaining Garmin test, to verify the manual **Run** playlist before selecting an episode for the writeback test.

- Start with a local, read-only script. Resolve the manual **Run** playlist to a stable ID and recover ordered episode UUIDs, podcast UUIDs, enclosure URLs, and played state. Third-party examples use `GET /user/playlists`, `POST /up_next/list` (for later use), and `POST /user/episode` for episode details. Check the actual response shapes, authentication, and expiry with a signed-in test account. The public MCP covers the catalog, and episode sharing supplies links. Account state requires the unofficial adapter. [shuffle-spread client](https://github.com/turquoise-turtle/shuffle-spread#what-it-talks-to) · [Music Assistant client](https://github.com/music-assistant/server/blob/dev/music_assistant/providers/pocketcasts/api_client.py) · [Pocket Casts MCP scope](https://support.pocketcasts.com/knowledge-base/pocket-casts-mcp-server-connect-claude-chatgpt-or-cursor/) · [Pocket Casts sharing](https://support.pocketcasts.com/knowledge-base/sharing-podcasts-and-episodes/)
- After reads work, deliberately choose one test episode and capture its pre-write state. Try the Music Assistant client's `POST /sync/update_episode` with episode `uuid`, `podcast` UUID, and `status: 3`. Read back played state and inspect the phone, playlist, and Up Next. Pocket Casts documents that Mark as Played removes an episode from Up Next. Verify whether the private endpoint has that side effect. Keep automatic writes off until verified. [Music Assistant played update](https://github.com/music-assistant/server/blob/dev/music_assistant/providers/pocketcasts/api_client.py) · [Pocket Casts queue behavior](https://support.pocketcasts.com/knowledge-base/playing-episodes-and-managing-your-queue/)
- **Exit criterion.** The read matches the app's playlist order and resolves audio for test episodes. The one deliberate write reads back as played with understood side effects. If reads fail, move the share/paste fallback forward. If writes fail, keep completion local. Isolate the adapter so it can be disabled or reconnected independently.

## 2. Normalize the queue and reuse uploads

- Build a small Cloudflare Worker adapter that merges the playlist and custom RSS uploads and filters episodes completed on either device. Return a versioned JSON manifest with stable item ID, source, display title, duration and bytes where known, and an HTTPS audio URL. Keep Pocket Casts UUID mappings server-side. Deduplicate verified matches using stable identity. Retain direct publisher enclosures where they play and proxy or normalize only reproducible incompatibilities.
- Inspect how the existing feed, storage, and uploader fit together before choosing bindings. Reuse the current storage. If it is R2, stream validated uploads into it and avoid buffering the full file in the Worker. Check size and format and serve actual MP3s as `audio/mpeg`. Use RSS or manifest titles via Garmin's `Media.ContentMetadata` so filenames and ID3 tags require no manual edits. Confirm existing hosting limits against real file sizes. [R2 Workers API](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/api/workers/workers-api-reference/) · [Garmin ContentMetadata](https://developer.garmin.com/connect-iq/api-docs/Toybox/Media/ContentMetadata.html)
- Store Pocket Casts credentials in Worker secrets. Use a revocable watch token for sync and separate private admin authentication for uploads. Keep credentials and sensitive feed or enclosure URLs out of logs. [Cloudflare Workers secrets](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/configuration/secrets/)
- **Exit criterion.** A known playlist episode and a private upload with an edited title both produce stable, ordered manifest entries with playable HTTPS audio. A second refresh yields the same IDs. The existing feed still works and the watch credential cannot administer uploads.

## 3. Make one-tap sync reliable

1. Persist a unique playback event ID and completion immediately for `SONG_EVENT_COMPLETE` or **Mark Finished**. Keep the local completion record separate from the download and retain pending events until acknowledged. Ordinary pause, skip, and delete never generate completion events.
2. Use `POST /v1/watch/sync` to submit queued events and receive acknowledged event IDs, the desired manifest, and separate Pocket Casts delivery status. In D1, deduplicate by event ID and commit event ingestion, the completion record, and any Pocket Casts outbox item together before acknowledging. A replay gets the same acknowledgement. The outbox only targets verified mappings, claims each pending item before delivery, reads played state before writing and after the attempt, and retries transient failures with backoff. On expired auth, retain the pending write and show a reconnect status. A failed playlist refresh uses the last good selection with local completions still filtered out. [Cloudflare D1 transactional batches](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/worker-api/d1-database/#batch)
3. Evict completed downloads and fetch missing audio sequentially over Wi-Fi. Base the budget on Garmin media-cache size and capacity, with a reserve measured on the watch. For unknown sizes, use a bounded transfer if supported or defer that item. Only expose a download after it succeeds. Clean up partial transfers and preserve in-progress and ready audio on errors. On a successful refresh, reconcile superseded downloads so queue changes cannot accumulate files indefinitely. Show what is playable, what is pending, and whether the last sync hit low space, lost Wi-Fi, or was canceled. [Garmin CacheStatistics](https://developer.garmin.com/connect-iq/api-docs/Toybox/Media/CacheStatistics.html)
4. **Exit criterion.** One Pocket Casts episode and one uploaded MP3 play offline. After the Pocket Casts episode finishes, one sync marks it played in Pocket Casts, frees its cache bytes, and pulls the next episode. Check a duplicate event after a lost response, an interrupted and retried download, cancellation, low space, expired Pocket Casts auth and reconnection, an MP3 that previously caused trouble, and a skipped episode that stays unplayed.

Test cache cleanup and retries when a download fails with **Storage Full** even though Windows reports free space. Windows free space and the provider's measured cache capacity may differ.

## 4. Add automation after one-tap sync works

- Refresh on demand first. A Worker Cron Trigger can retry pending Pocket Casts writes or precompute the queue on the server. The cron job cannot wake the watch. Test Garmin's `onActivityCompleted` after saving a Run and reconnecting for a small metadata upload. Separately test charging and Wi-Fi on the 255S Music to see whether it can download audio unattended. Retain **Sync Now** either way. [Cloudflare Cron Triggers](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/configuration/cron-triggers/) · [Garmin Background](https://developer.garmin.com/connect-iq/api-docs/Toybox/Background.html) · [Garmin ServiceDelegate](https://developer.garmin.com/connect-iq/api-docs/Toybox/System/ServiceDelegate.html)
- Add **Share → Add to Run** through a phone share target or an authenticated paste page. Resolve episode links to publisher RSS enclosures and test with a representative set of feeds. If identity is ambiguous, ask for a selection or leave the episode unlinked, with local played tracking. [Pocket Casts episode sharing](https://support.pocketcasts.com/knowledge-base/sharing-podcasts-and-episodes/)
- Keep completion-only sync until real-hardware `ActiveContent` resume works for the audio sources, differences from dynamic ads are understood, and stale watch updates cannot overwrite newer phone progress. [Garmin ActiveContent report](https://forums.garmin.com/developer/connect-iq/i/bug-reports/music-playback-from-custom-position-with-activecontent-not-working)
- **Exit criterion.** Enable each automation only after repeated real-device trials verify it, including recovery from lost connectivity. If automatic audio sync proves unreliable, the one-tap workflow is the finished daily path.
